What Is Talent Pool and Bench Strength Software?

Talent pool software helps organizations organize groups of internal employees who may be relevant to future roles, workforce requirements, or succession planning.

Bench strength describes the depth of internal talent available to support future workforce needs—especially when important or critical roles need successors. TraineryHCM's Talent environment includes talent pools, bench strength, coverage by department, succession coverage and readiness-related visibility.

Bench strength refers to the depth of internal talent available to support future roles and workforce needs. In succession planning, stronger bench strength generally means an organization has greater internal talent depth to consider when important roles need coverage.
