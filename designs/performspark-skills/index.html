⚡ FAQs

Your questions answered

What is skills management software?⌄
Skills management software helps organizations capture, assess, organize, and develop workforce capabilities. PerformSpark connects skills information with evidence, validation, role readiness, development, career paths, and succession.
What is the difference between skills management software and skills matrix software?⌄
A skills matrix typically shows who has which skills and at what level. Skills management software adds evidence, validation, freshness, gap analysis, development, career progression, and workforce planning workflows.
Can managers validate employee skills?⌄
Yes. Managers have team skills visibility and a validation queue.
Can PerformSpark identify skill gaps and role readiness?⌄
PerformSpark includes skill targets, certifications, readiness views, and skills readiness by role.
How do skill gaps connect to employee development?⌄
Skills information can inform team learning plans, IDPs, growth plans, goals, learning, and coaching workflows.
Does PerformSpark support career pathing and succession planning?⌄
Yes. Career paths, aspirations, role exploration, career ladders, advancement criteria, critical roles, succession pipelines, talent pools, and bench strength are supported.
