HCM’s Hall of Shame: Seven Mistakes That Won’t Go Away

Mahesh Kumar, Founder & Managing Director of TraineryHCM, joins David Wentworth of Brandon Hall Group to unpack seven persistent HCM mistakes — and what HR leaders can do differently.

Key takeaways

Seven HCM mistakes that keep repeating

The episode connects everyday HR operating problems to the downstream impact they create across talent, performance, learning, compensation and workforce decisions.

01

Treating job descriptions like historical documents

When the job description falls behind the real job, hiring, pay, performance standards, skills and development can all start from the wrong foundation.

Job Description Management →
02

Confusing the annual review with performance management

An annual documentation event is not the same as continuous coaching, feedback, check-ins and performance conversations.

Check-Ins & 1-on-1s →
03

Rewarding results while ignoring behaviors

Outcome-only metrics can reinforce the wrong behavior when leaders do not examine how the result was achieved.

360 Feedback →
04

Buying more software instead of connecting it

Best-of-breed point solutions can turn into “best of chaos” when workforce data and processes remain fragmented.

HCM Integrations →
05

Delivering one-size-fits-all training

Different functions, career stages and goals require relevant learning rather than the same bulk catalog for everyone.

Connected Learning →
06

Promoting top performers without management readiness

High performance in an individual role does not automatically mean someone is ready for the fundamentally different work of managing people.

Succession Planning →
07

Managing by gut feel instead of workforce intelligence

Talent decisions become more defensible when performance, succession, assessment, learning and skills context are considered together.

Talent Management →
“If the foundation is wrong, everything you build on it is going to be wrong.”
Mahesh Kumar · Founder & Managing Director, TraineryHCM
