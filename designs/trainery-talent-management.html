Talent Management FAQs

Your Questions Answered

Talent management software helps organizations structure processes related to future talent, career progression, workforce skills, internal talent pipelines, and succession. Trainery Talent brings these workflows together around Talent Review, Succession Planning, Career Path, Skills Management, and Talent Pools & Bench Strength.

A performance review evaluates how an employee is performing in the current role. A talent review focuses on future potential, readiness, risk, and possible organizational roles. In TraineryHCM, PerformSpark owns performance management while Trainery Talent owns future-talent workflows.

Succession planning software helps HR identify critical roles, organize potential successors, assess readiness, and understand where succession coverage may be limited across departments or pipelines.

Career pathing software makes possible future roles and progression requirements visible to employees. Trainery Talent supports career paths, career ladders, aspirations, role exploration, advancement criteria, open positions, and career approvals.

Skills data helps HR and managers understand workforce capability and readiness. Skill profiles, evidence, validation, targets, and role-readiness views can add context to career and succession decisions without replacing the dedicated Career Path or Succession Planning workflows.
